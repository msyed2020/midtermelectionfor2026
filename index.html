    What's here

      Sources

      Nominees and ratings: Wikipedia's 2026 Senate and 2026 gubernatorial election articles, as of October 13, 2026, with Cook Political Report and Sabato's Crystal Ball ratings as listed there. Late changes (Maine's replacement nominee, South Carolina's special runoff, Alaska's ballot dispute) were checked against news coverage.

      Issue stances: paraphrased from the nonpartisan voter guides at guides.vote, which cite candidates' votes, bills and public statements. The short labels (“Ban”, “Tighten”, “Mostly yes”…) are guides.vote's own. Every race page links to its source guide.

      Caveats

      • Stance text was extracted and condensed from the source guides with AI assistance. It is a summary, not a quotation; check the linked guide (and its citations) before relying on any specific claim.
      • Detailed stances exist only for races guides.vote has covered: 11 Senate, 8 governor and 32 competitive House races. All other Senate and governor races show nominees, incumbency and ratings, with a Ballotpedia link.
      • “No position found” means the source guide found no stated position, not that the candidate has none.
      • The House section covers competitive districts only, not all 435 seats.
